## 🛠️ How to Enable Unknown Sources on Android Auto

To display patched applications on your vehicle screen, you must enable Developer Settings in Android Auto:

1. **Open Android Auto Settings:** Search for "Android Auto" in your phone's Settings app.
2. **Unlock Developer Mode:** Scroll down to the bottom and **tap the "Version" section 10 times consecutively**. Tap **OK** on the pop-up confirmation.
3. **Open Developer Settings:** Tap the **three-dot menu (⋮)** in the top-right corner &rarr; **Developer settings**.
4. **Enable Unknown Sources:** Locate the **Unknown sources** checkbox and turn it on.
