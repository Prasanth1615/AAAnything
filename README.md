## 💬 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

- **Q. What are the device requirements?**  
  An Android smartphone running **Android 15 or higher**. No root access, bootloader unlocking, or computer is needed.
- **Q. Does this modify my car's system or void my vehicle warranty?**  
  Not at all. AA Anything operates purely on your smartphone using standard Android Auto communication, leaving your vehicle's software completely untouched.
- **Q. Can every single app be projected?**  
  While a wide variety of apps work great, apps that rely on OS-level DRM copyright protection, signature tamper detection, or Play Integrity checks cannot be projected due to Android platform security rules.
- **Q. A patched app stopped launching / opening. What should I do?**  
  An Android system or security update may have temporarily prevented the app from opening. **Do NOT uninstall the app**—simply re-patch it in-place using **AA Anything**. In most cases, it will open normally again while keeping all your data and login sessions intact.
